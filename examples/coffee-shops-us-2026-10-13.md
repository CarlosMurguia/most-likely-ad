## 2. The numbers
- Top structures: framed · top centered (6 of 25), full-bleed · top centered (6 of 25), framed · no words (5 of 25).
- Chance: spread 25 ads over 14 structures at random and the fullest would hold about 4; here two structures hold 6 each, and the two together hold 12 where chance gives about 8.
- Words: top centered in 12 of 25. The next position used more than twice is "no words at all" (8 of 25, mostly coffee bags and merch on white).
- Color: orange in 18 ads, brown in 13, red in 9. Purple in none.
- Type: display or condensed sans in 11 of the 17 ads with a headline; all caps in 11 of 17.
- Other: 9:16 in 14 of 25; product isolated in 15; people in 2; promo sticker in 5.

## 3. Where nobody is
- Structures: a full-bleed photo with the words at the side, in the middle or at the bottom (0 of 25); framed with the words bottom-side (0 of 25).
- Colors: purple absent; green and pink in 2 ads each.
- People: a person at more than incidental scale appears in 2 of 25.

## 4. Counter-brief

**Direction 1 — The moment, words low**
Breaks: headline top-centered (12 of 25) and the isolated product (15 of 25).
Keeps: the cup is still in the frame, 9:16, warm light.
Do: full-bleed photo of the moment around the coffee (the counter at 7 a.m., a hand reaching), headline in a left-aligned column in the bottom third. One type size.
Watch: keep the bottom 15% clear of the platform's buttons in Stories/Reels placements.

**Direction 2 — A person, not a cup**
Breaks: no people (23 of 25).
Keeps: orange/brown palette, the product present.
Do: one person at half body or closer, the drink incidental in their hand; headline in sentence case, not caps.
Watch: real customers or staff photograph better here than a styled model; avoid stock-looking smiles.

**Direction 3 — Off-palette**
Breaks: orange/brown palette (18 and 13 of 25) and all caps (11 of 17).
Keeps: the structure everyone uses, so the ad is still easy to place.
Do: one color the category doesn't use (green or purple, if the brand allows), a serif headline in sentence case, no promo sticker.
Watch: this is the easiest direction to copy; it buys distinctiveness for one season, not for good.

## 5. Limits
- 25 ads is a snapshot of what was running on October 13, 2026, in the order the library served it (biggest spenders first).
- One platform only (Meta).
- This measures how alike the ads are built, not whether they work.
